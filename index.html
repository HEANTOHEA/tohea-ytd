Quick Guide

How to download YouTube Music to MP3

Copy a link, paste it above, and save tracks in seconds — works on phone and desktop browsers.

01

Copy Music Link

Open YouTube or YouTube Music app / website. Click Share on any song, video or playlist and click Copy Link.

02

Paste the URL

Paste the copied link into the field at the top of this page and click Download.

03

Pick quality

Choose your preferred bitrate (320 Kbps MP3 recommended) from the dropdown under the input.

04

Save MP3 or ZIP

Click Download MP3 on each track, or use ZIP Download for entire playlists.
